# Concept Note Market List: Recheck and Ambiguity Audit

Reviewed: 27 October 2026.

**Handling:** Internal working review. The original concept note footer reads `UNDP Sudan Confidential`; this audit does not remove that marking or authorize public reuse of its contents.

## Finding

The original Arabic Word document states an intended sample of **15 markets**, but contains **14 populated market-list paragraphs**. No separate fifteenth market name can be identified with confidence from the document.

There is a material alternative explanation: the seventh paragraph combines **سوق أم درمان للمواشي** and **سوق الإبل (سوق المواشي)**. If these were intended as two distinct sites, the list could represent 15 markets despite having only 14 list paragraphs. If they are alternative descriptions of one site, the document contains only 14 distinguishable entries and the omitted market is unnamed. The note does not resolve which interpretation is correct.

Accordingly, **the missing market cannot responsibly be named**. Neither an unlisted familiar market nor a split of this combined line should be adopted simply to make the count equal 15.

## Interpretation of the Table

- Arabic entries preserve the original market-list wording, including place labels and malformed text; only surrounding whitespace is removed.
- English names are working transliterations, not verified official names. Parenthetical aliases are explicitly identified rather than silently substituted for the source.
- **Locality** distinguishes the location label actually used by the concept note from a verified formal locality assignment. The words Khartoum, Omdurman and Bahri can refer to wider urban areas; the concept note does not prove a locality PCode or boundary match.
- **Administrative unit** means a formal sub-locality administrative unit, not the state or an informal neighborhood. No such units are specified for these entries.
- Confidence describes interpretation/identification from the wording: high for a reasonably recognizable name; medium for a generic/variant name requiring resolution; low for malformed or potentially merged entries. **All coordinates and formal administrative assignments remain unverified.**

The most useful document-owner clarification is: **Does paragraph 26 refer to two different markets? If yes, what are their separate names and existing location records? If not, which additional market was intended to complete the 15?** This is clarification of an existing document, not a new market-condition survey.

Request the approved formal locality and sub-locality administrative-unit names/codes for every site, and the intended wording for `سوق المحالي`, `سوق الخيمة`, and `بحري سعد بحري`. Do not infer the missing market from unrepresented localities; the note's intended all-locality coverage does not identify a unique omitted site.
