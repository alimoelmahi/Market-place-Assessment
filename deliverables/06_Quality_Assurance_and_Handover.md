## 3. Required Before Investment or Publication

- Security and explosive-ordnance, land/permission, engineering, environmental/social, and economic/O&M gates require appropriate current documentary support. Unknown cannot become a pass because a model score is high. Required future inspections and clearances remain outside this secondary-only assessment.
- Confirm an evidenced deficit, additionality, viable utilization, fair access, maintenance funding, and no-build/service alternatives for each candidate intervention.
- Obtain permission for the supplied StoryMap's classified/uncleared material, photographs, quotations, source services, and any sensitive market records before reuse. The reference-review and translation note is internal working material unless separately cleared.
- Review licenses at resource level; GADM, ACLED, GSMA, and commercial imagery cannot be assumed freely redistributable. Building-derived products and basemaps retain applicable attribution/rights.
- Verify ArcGIS Pro/Online/Experience Builder/StoryMaps access, extensions, publishing privileges, storage and credit budget. No actual ArcGIS item, service, dashboard, or StoryMap has been published by this task.
- Test actual data joins, scenario totals, independent interpretation agreement, public field/record restrictions, anonymous access, mobile readability, Arabic rendering, accessibility, and low-bandwidth fallbacks after implementation.
- Obtain professional bilingual/editorial review and organizational approval before donor-facing or public release. The translation is a faithful working translation, not a certified translation or verification of its author's factual claims.

## 4. Decision Integrity

Do not represent this package as a completed 15-market survey or a comprehensive census of all major markets. Do not copy fictional MCDA examples into result layers. Do not publish exact safe-route, clearance, trader-demographic, income, employment, turnover, or food-safety claims unsupported by suitable evidence.

Where secondary evidence cannot resolve a question, retain a named evidence gap, provide bounded scenarios where defensible, and explain which decision must remain conditional. That is a valid assessment result, not a reason to manufacture a complete-looking map or rank.
